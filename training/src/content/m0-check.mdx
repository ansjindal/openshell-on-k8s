## What you learned

- One VM runs the whole lab: k3s, the agent-sandbox controller, the OpenShell gateway, LiteLLM, Keycloak, the OpenShell Console and the monitoring stack.
- A sandbox is a pair of pods plus a policy: the agent's workload pod and a supervisor pod that owns its network.
- The gateway is the control plane: it creates sandboxes, stores policies and provider credentials, and checks every call against the caller's identity and role.
- Landlock and seccomp bound the agent process; these static parts are fixed when the sandbox is created.
- The network is deny by default, filtered at L4 (host, port, binary) and L7 (method, path), and its rules can change on a running sandbox.
- Providers keep credentials in the gateway; the agent only sees a placeholder, and every decision is written as an OCSF audit event.
