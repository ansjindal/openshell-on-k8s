## What you learned

- The fleet is four read-only specialists (logs, metrics, traces, events), each with egress to exactly one backend, plus an analyst with no egress at all.
- `fleet.txt` describes the whole fleet, one agent per whitespace-separated line, and `scripts/fleet.sh up` creates each sandbox, scopes its policy and stages its persona.
- Sealed agents read their in-cluster backend through the `cluster-telemetry` skill, which shells out to `curl` under the agent's egress policy, because `web_fetch` refuses internal addresses.
- The orchestrator runs a fixed plan with one prescribed probe per specialist, then hands all findings to the analyst for a root cause and a recommended fix.
- In the incident lab you take `payments` down for real; the fleet diagnoses it from live telemetry and a human approves the fix.
- Extending the fleet is always the same three moves: add an egress line, add a persona, add a probe.
