A quick look back at Module 2 before you move on.

## What you learned

- Agents reach a model through LiteLLM via the `litellm` provider: the sandbox holds only a placeholder, and the supervisor injects the real key on model calls the profile allows.
- A provider profile is the access contract: which variable the sandbox gets, where the credential may go, and which requests are allowed there. Anything else, like LiteLLM's admin API, is refused at L7.
- You can build your own provider, attach it to a running sandbox, rotate its token and detach it, all without a restart.
- An agent's persona is plain Markdown in its workspace (`IDENTITY.md`, `SOUL.md`, `BOOTSTRAP.md`), re-read each session.
- Skills are governed three ways: the registry they come from, whether they are enabled, and what they can reach.
- `./scripts/fleet task` hands a prompt to one agent or the whole fleet, each working in its own sandbox.
