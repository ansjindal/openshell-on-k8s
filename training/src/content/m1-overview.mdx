<Optional title="Why one supervisor pod per sandbox?">
The supervisor holds the policy and the credentials, and the workload pod's NetworkPolicy lets it talk to its own supervisor only. Keeping them in separate pods means a compromised agent process shares no kernel namespace, no filesystem and no secrets with the component that decides what it may reach.
</Optional>
