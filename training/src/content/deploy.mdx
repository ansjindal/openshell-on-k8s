Good news: **the VM is already provisioned.** Ansible (`./scripts/setup.sh`) installed k3s, the agent-sandbox controller, LiteLLM, cert-manager, Keycloak, the OpenShell gateway, the console and the monitoring stack when the machine came up. You don't stand anything up here; you **confirm it's healthy**.

<Step n="2" title="Ask who you are">

The gateway accepts no anonymous calls. Ask it who you are:

```bash
openshell whoami
```
<ExpectedOutput>
{`Current User

  Subject: lab-operator
  Name: lab-operator
  Provider: mtls
  Roles: operator`}
</ExpectedOutput>

This shell presents a client certificate with `OU=operator`, which cert-manager issued for the VM. An operator is a platform admin across every workspace. People in the browser sign in with Keycloak instead; you'll meet them in [Users, Roles & Workspaces](/learn/access-control).
